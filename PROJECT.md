# 🌱 Plant Shop Project

## 1. Vision

Build a plant shop web application that goes beyond simply selling plants. The website should help people discover plants that fit their lifestyle and environment, then help them understand how to care for those plants properly.

The application should feel like a real, engaging plant business while using plant discovery and plant education as its main differentiators.

---

## 2. Project North Star

> **Build a plant shop that helps people discover plants suited to their lives and understand how to care for them properly.**

Every major feature and design decision should support this goal.

---

## 3. Core Goal

Create an engaging and interactive plant shop where users can:

1. Browse and explore plants.
2. Find plants that match their needs through a Plant Finder.
3. Learn how to properly care for the plants they discover.

The project should prioritize depth and quality in these areas rather than adding many unrelated features.

---

## 4. Target Users

The primary audience is people who are interested in plants, including:

- Beginners who do not know which plants are suitable for them.
- People who want plants for specific spaces such as bedrooms, desks, balconies, or gardens.
- People who are curious about plants and want to learn more.
- Existing plant owners who want simple, understandable care information.

---

# 5. Core Features

## 5.1 🌿 Plant Shop

The application remains fundamentally a plant shop.

Users should be able to:

- Browse available plants.
- Search for plants.
- Filter or categorize plants.
- View plant information.
- View prices.
- Open individual plant/product pages.

The shop provides the business foundation of the application.

---

## 5.2 🌱 Plant Finder

The Plant Finder is the first major differentiating feature.

Its purpose is to help users discover plants instead of expecting them to already know what plant they want.

Users answer simple questions about factors such as:

- Where they want to place the plant.
- Available sunlight.
- How much care they are willing to provide.
- Their experience level.
- Their preferences or needs.

The system uses those answers to recommend suitable plants.

### Main experience

User answers questions
→ system evaluates their choices
→ suitable plants are recommended
→ user explores the recommended plant
→ user can continue to the plant shop or care information.

The recommendation experience should feel helpful, simple, and engaging rather than like a complicated questionnaire.

---

## 5.3 🪴 Plant Care

Plant Care is the second major differentiating feature.

Its purpose is to help users understand how to properly care for the plants they discover or purchase.

Plant care information may include:

- Light requirements.
- Watering guidance.
- Soil information.
- Temperature/environment.
- Difficulty level.
- Common care mistakes.
- General care tips.

The information should be presented in an engaging and easy-to-understand way rather than as a long block of text.

The goal is to make learning plant care approachable, especially for beginners.

---

# 6. Core User Journey

The main experience should connect the three core areas:

**Discover → Find → Learn → Shop**

Example:

1. User enters the website.
2. User explores the plant shop or chooses Plant Finder.
3. User answers Plant Finder questions.
4. The system recommends suitable plants.
5. User opens a recommended plant.
6. User learns about the plant and its care requirements.
7. User can return to the shop and explore/purchase the plant.

The features should feel connected rather than like separate pages placed together.

---

# 7. Scope

## We ARE building

- A plant shop interface.
- Plant browsing.
- Plant search/filtering.
- Plant detail pages.
- Plant Finder questionnaire.
- Plant recommendation logic.
- Plant Care information.
- Engaging interactions and visual feedback.
- Responsive web design.
- A polished and cohesive plant-focused visual identity.

## We are NOT currently building

- Complex logistics/delivery systems.
- Real-time inventory infrastructure.
- Payment processing unless later required.
- A social media/community platform.
- Complex AI plant identification.
- A charity/planting organization system.
- A full medical/scientific plant diagnosis system.
- A large-scale marketplace with multiple independent sellers.

These may be considered later, but they are not part of the current core project.

---

# 8. Design Direction

The website should feel:

- Fresh.
- Natural.
- Welcoming.
- Modern.
- Calm.
- Interactive.
- Beginner-friendly.

The interface should use plant imagery, natural visual elements, clear information hierarchy, smooth interactions, and meaningful animations where appropriate.

Animations should support the experience rather than exist only for decoration.

The design should make users want to explore plants.

---

# 9. Product Philosophy

### Sell + Discover + Teach

The website is not only a catalog.

**Plant Shop** gives users products to explore.

**Plant Finder** helps users discover what may suit them.

**Plant Care** helps users understand what they are choosing and how to look after it.

The combination is the core identity of the project.

---

---

# 11. Development Principle

Prioritize:

**Quality > Quantity**

A smaller number of well-designed, functional features is better than many unfinished features.

The three core areas are:

1. Plant Shop
2. Plant Finder
3. Plant Care

These should receive the majority of development and design attention.
